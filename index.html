    Into Shanxi, 1218

    Genghis Khan has gone west and left Muqali to fight the Jin. Take his column from Datong to Taiyuan across the mountain passes, and keep the horses alive until spring.

    • Horses only eat by grazing: every hour on the road is an hour less to eat.
    • Forced marches pay off for two or three days, then tired horses slow down and die.
    • Sheep are food on the hoof, but the column moves at their pace.
    • Winter grass is scarce and poor. A camp that stays put eats the grass around it.
